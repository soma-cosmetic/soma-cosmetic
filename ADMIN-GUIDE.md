# How to edit your catalog (no code, works from a phone)

Your admin panel is **Pages CMS**: https://app.pagescms.org

Everything below works from a phone browser. After you press **Save**,
the live site updates itself automatically — wait about **1 minute**, then
refresh the site to see the change.

## Logging in

1. Open **https://app.pagescms.org** in your phone's browser.
2. Tap **Sign in with GitHub** and log in with the shop's GitHub account
   (created during setup — see README).
3. Pick the **soma-cosmetic** repository. You'll see four sections:
   **Shop Settings, Products, Categories, Brands.**

Tip: use your phone's "Add to Home Screen" so the admin panel opens like
an app.

### Giving your team access (no GitHub needed for them)

In Pages CMS, open the site's **Settings → Collaborators** and invite
teammates by **email**. They get a magic sign-in link — no GitHub account
required.

## Add a product

1. Open **Products** → tap **Add** (the + button on the product list).
2. Fill in: product name, brand (pick from the list), category (pick from
   the list), price (numbers only, e.g. `8500` — leave empty to show
   "Ask for price").
3. Tap the **Photo** field → choose a photo from your phone or camera.
4. Tap **Save**. Done — live in about a minute.

To **edit** a product, tap it in the list, change the fields, Save.
To **delete** one, open it and use the remove/trash option, then Save.

## Change the shop name, logo, tagline, or contact info

1. Open **Shop Settings**.
2. Change what you need:
   - **Shop name** and **Tagline** — the text at the top of the site
   - **Logo image** — upload a square image (or leave empty for the
     simple "S" monogram)
   - **Phone / WhatsApp number** — WhatsApp must be digits only with
     country code, e.g. `9647501234567`
   - **Address, Instagram, Facebook** links
3. Tap **Save**.

## Add or rename a category or brand

1. Open **Categories** (or **Brands**) → **Add entry** → type the name → Save.
2. The new name now appears in the dropdown when you add/edit products.
3. A category only shows on the website once **at least one product** uses it.
4. **Renaming:** if you rename a category or brand, also open the products
   that used the old name and re-pick the new one (the site lists exactly
   what's written on each product).

## Photo tips

- Square photos look best (the site crops to a square).
- Don't worry about photo size — the site automatically shrinks big phone
  photos so pages stay fast.

## If something looks wrong

Every change is saved in GitHub's history, so nothing is ever truly lost —
changes can be rolled back. Contact whoever set up the site (or ask Claude)
to restore a previous version.
